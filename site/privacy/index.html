Privacy Policy

Last updated September 10, 2026

Yay Everybody is built to let people play games with friends. We collect only the information needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.

Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, this may include account information such as your name and profile image, game activity and content you submit, invitation and multiplayer information, device and technical information, and notification tokens when you enable push notifications.

How we use information

We use information to provide accounts and gameplay, connect players, deliver notifications, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, maintain security, and improve Yay Everybody.

Service providers

We may use third-party hosting, database, authentication, analytics, and notification providers to operate the service. Those providers may process information on our behalf subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or to vendors that help us operate Yay Everybody.

Data choices

You can choose whether to enable notifications and can request help with account or privacy questions through our Support page.

Children

Yay Everybody is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take steps to delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The latest version will always be posted here.

Contact support with privacy questions.
